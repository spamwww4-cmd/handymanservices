Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does a fridge repair cost?

It depends on the fault. A regas is far cheaper than a compressor replacement. We diagnose first and give you a fixed quote before any work starts — the quote is free.

My fridge is running but not cold. What's wrong?

Usually one of three things: a gas leak, a blockage in the sealed system, or a failing compressor. All three are exactly what we test for on a first visit.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes — every repair carries a 6-month workmanship guarantee. If the same fault returns, we come back and make it right.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Bellville and cover the greater Cape Town northern suburbs — Bellville, Durbanville, Parow, Goodwood, Kraaifontein, Brackenfell and surrounds. Ask if you're unsure.

Is it worth repairing, or should I buy new?

If the compressor, gas system and cabinet are sound, a repair is almost always cheaper than replacement. We'll give you an honest answer either way.
